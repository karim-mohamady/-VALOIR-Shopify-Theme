# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does Valoir require external apps for 3D or AR?
No. Valoir natively integrates with Shopify's built-in 3D model viewer (`model_viewer_tag`) and WebGL canvas. External apps are completely optional.

### Is Arabic and RTL supported out of the box?
Yes. Valoir includes native `ar.json` translations and automatic `dir="rtl"` layout adaptations for all headers, mega-menus, drawers, product galleries, and checkout routes.

### How do I configure prescription optical notes?
Enable `enable_cart_notes` in Theme Settings > Cart & Checkout. The cart drawer and cart page will automatically render the prescription and pupillary distance input field.
