# Valoir 3D Product Experience System

## Philosophy
The 3D experience in Valoir is an optical enhancer, not a heavy WebGL gimmick. It loads on-demand without blocking the initial critical rendering path of the Shopify storefront.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Shopify Product Model Integration**: Fully supports `.glb` and `.usdz` assets uploaded via standard Shopify Admin.
2. **Interactive Orbit Controls**: Smooth touch and mouse rotation with damped inertia, clampable tilt angles, and pinch/wheel zoom.
3. **Finish & Acetate Swatch Reactivity**: When a customer changes variants, the 3D viewer dynamically re-tints the frame materials and lens reflection coatings.
4. **Caliper Scale Overlay**: Micro-caliper measurement guides (bridge, frame width, temple length) visually map real-world millimeters onto the 3D model.
5. **Native AR Launch**: Apple QuickLook and WebXR SceneViewer bridges allow customers to inspect frames in their physical room or on a desktop stand.
