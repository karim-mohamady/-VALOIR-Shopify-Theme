# Valoir Design System & Aesthetic Foundations

## Palette
- **Base Canvas**: `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster Warm White)
- **Deep Neutral**: `#1A1A1A` (Architectural Charcoal Obsidian)
- **Muted Subtext**: `#6B6864` (Warm Optical Stone)
- **Precious Accent**: `#C29B38` (Sabae Antique Champagne Gold)
- **Divider Hairline**: `#E5E2DC` (Subtle Warm Border)
- **Sale / Special Badge**: `#8B261E` (Oxblood Lacquer)

## Typography Hierarchy
- **Display & Headings**: *Cormorant Garamond* (Low-contrast optical serif with humanist curves)
- **Functional Body & Specs**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Geometric clean readability at 14px-16px)
- **Arabic / RTL**: *Noto Sans Arabic* (Balanced baseline with tailored typographic rhythm)

## Spatial System
- Page Max Width: `1440px`
- Container Padding: `2rem` (desktop), `1.25rem` (mobile)
- Corner Radius System: `2px` (micro), `4px` (cards & inputs), `8px` (modals)
- Strict Anti-Slop: No purple/blue gradients, no heavy glassmorphism, no artificial floating pills.
