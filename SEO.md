# Search Engine Optimization & Structured Data Standards

- **Semantic HTML5**: Native `header`, `nav`, `main`, `section`, `article`, and `footer` elements.
- **Product Schema**: Injected JSON-LD format with `@type: Product`, offer price, currency, availability, and high-res media.
- **Canonical URLs**: Dynamic Shopify `canonical_url` tag prevents duplicate indexing across collections.
- **OpenGraph & Twitter**: Fully dynamic social share card metadata with fallback to shop description and high-resolution logo/product photography.
